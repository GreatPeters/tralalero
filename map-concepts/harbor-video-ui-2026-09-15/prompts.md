Tool: built-in `image_gen`. Two separate generation calls using the approved Harbor UI board as style reference and the actual-v3 story screenshot as content/control reference. Outputs are static concept images, not new videos or exact native playback captures.

## A — cinema immersion

Use case: ui-mockup. Create CONCEPT A, a polished portrait mobile game STORY VIDEO SCREEN, called "cinema immersion" in design notes but DO NOT write that concept name inside the app. Output portrait 1024x2048.
Input 1 is the user's APPROVED Harbor Workshop game UI, a STYLE reference only: dark worn wood, thin brass rivets, parchment and warm gold buttons.
Input 2 is the CURRENT real video screen, a CONTENT and CONTROLS reference: a PORTRAIT 9:16 movie of a natural shark carrying one golden sneaker in its mouth, leaping through bright blue harbor water next to a shrine. Preserve that exact narrative, subject, movie aspect and current visible framing. This is the first scene BEFORE it gains legs, so do not invent legs, walking shoes or the shop mascot in the movie. Preserve the tiny existing video watermark inside the movie if visible; don't invent new watermarks.

CONCEPT A composition: maximum video presence with only restrained 2D overlays. The movie is the dominant ~80% of the device height, a tall 9:16 rectangle fitted fully without stretching or cropping. The portrait movie frame has a very thin dark-brass rim and little decoration. Narrow dark wood strips at screen top/bottom use the same material as the approved shop. No thick cabinet, no ornate theater curtains, no phone mockup.
TOP safe-area strip: small left text "이야기 01 / 04", compact right dark wood button "건너뛰기".
MOVIE: bright clear blue sea and the natural shark/sneaker action matching input2. Keep subject unobscured. Fit the whole portrait film. No gray or blank video placeholder and no large center play button because it is already playing.
LOWER VIDEO overlay on the WATER below the shark, a restrained translucent dark gradient just for readable captions: small warm-gold scene title "훔친 신발"; two cream subtitle lines EXACTLY "신단의 신발을 물고," / "상어는 항구를 빠져나갔다." These float over the moving picture instead of occupying a giant parchment card. Clear readable Korean rounded bold game type.
BELOW MOVIE: one very thin brass/gold progress rail, filled about a quarter, with no invented movie duration numbers.
BOTTOM safe-area action row: small subtle brown button "다시 보기" and larger warm gold primary button "다음 장면". Same wood/brass/parchment button craft as the approved UI but restrained. No wallet, coins, gems, health bar, shop icons or gameplay bottom nav on this cinematic screen. No giant illustration outside the movie. No new story or extra characters.
Render the completed clean UI as one flat front-on portrait screenshot, visually balanced and production-quality.

## B — harbor journal

Use case: ui-mockup. Create CONCEPT B, a polished portrait mobile STORY VIDEO SCREEN for the same Korean game, designed like a harbor travel journal mounted in a workshop. Output portrait 1024x2048.
Input1: APPROVED Harbor Workshop UI style reference, dark aged timber, brass frames/bolts, warm parchment and gold buttons.
Input2: CURRENT real story-video content and existing control reference. The movie is PORTRAIT 9:16: a natural shark holding a golden sneaker in its mouth and leaping in a bright blue fishing harbor beside a small shrine. It is before the curse, so no legs, no shoes on feet, no anthropomorphic shop shark. Keep the same shot's narrative and whole portrait composition, and preserve the tiny existing video watermark if present.

This is a DISTINCT alternative to an edge-to-edge cinematic screen: the whole layout feels like a carefully arranged warm parchment STORY JOURNAL on a dark timber backing, with the video inside a defined framed window and a separate readable caption card BELOW it. The film is still large, but the surrounding story framing has a deliberate hierarchy. Clear warm wood/brass/parchment materials exactly related to the approved shop, not a generic modern app, not a real open book with folded pages.
TOP safe area: a compact wooden title plaque "탈라 스토리", and a modest upper-right dark button "건너뛰기".
Under title a fine scene label "이야기 01 / 04".
MAIN VIDEO WINDOW: a tall 9:16 screen, fitted in full with no stretching/cropping and a restrained rounded brass border; keep bright blue movie colors unchanged. Occupies roughly 60-65% of total screen height and about 72% of screen width, centered. Slight dark margin or subtle parchment mat around it rather than a second generated background. Do NOT crop the portrait video into a landscape window. Do NOT overlay title or captions on the video, and no center play button.
BELOW VIDEO a separate full-width worn parchment caption card with metal/brass corner details, clear dark brown text: bold scene title "훔친 신발", followed by the two lines "신단의 신발을 물고," / "상어는 항구를 빠져나갔다." Comfortable text spacing, no decorative material behind the letters. Caption card should be large enough to read easily on a phone.
Below the caption card place a simple four-part story progress strip with only the first short segment gold and the other three dark muted brass. This is the existing four-scene story, not upgrade levels. No thumbnail gallery and no invented seek controls.
BOTTOM controls: smaller dark-wood "다시 보기" button left, larger golden parchment "다음 장면" button right. Both clear, generous mobile touch targets. Same brass outline and craftsmanship as the approved upgrade/shop buttons.
No wallet, coins, gems, health bars, shop cards or gameplay nav. No clocks, film-reel decorations, fake reel holes, new lore, floating props or extra characters. Balanced clean portrait composition, text fully legible, nothing clipped. The main difference from a cinema layout is that narrative text lives on its OWN parchment card and the video has clear space around its frame. Render one flat complete screen with no device mockup or explanatory annotations.
