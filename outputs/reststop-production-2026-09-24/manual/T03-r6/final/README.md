# T03 toilet

The final GLB and FBX contain 14,750 triangles. The editable Blender file keeps the accepted high source hidden as Detailed_Source and the deliverable as Asset_Optimized.

The original three AI shape attempts and two failed canonical reductions remain consumed. The bowl was recovered from cached stage161, textured once, and its rounded exterior normal field was repaired without moving source geometry. A separate local reconstruction of the open surface was required after both canonical reductions produced shards. The accepted reconstruction uses unsigned occupancy, one-cell surface thickening at a 0.0025-height pitch, two-cell closing and a smooth isosurface; its original PBR and normal field were then baked onto the rebuilt low mesh. No extra AI request was used for that reconstruction.

The open bowl is verified in both export formats. Surface-distance evidence describes sampled fidelity before baking, not an exhaustive Hausdorff guarantee. Small edges are rounded and glossy undersides appear dark in the studio. Geometry-only views expose shading that the retained normal map compensates. See validation records and the full comparison sheet. The final model is a static prop; no scene was changed.
